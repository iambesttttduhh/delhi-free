Diwali Art Mela

Paintings by teenagers with autism, Down syndrome and neurodivergent traits — the work itself is the subject, and the workshops are taught by the artists.

When
Sat 31 Oct 2:00 pm – Sun 1 Nov 2026
Where
Cure India, A-30, Block A, Gulmohar Park, New Delhi 110049
Metro
Green Park · Yellow · ~11 min walk
Entry
Free entry. Workshops ₹250 — register on arrival, 8800020511.
Neighbourhood
Gulmohar Park

What happens

Paintings hung along the walls with the young artists standing beside their own work, and workshop tables where they show you how they paint. You can buy, watch, or join a session.

Run of show

  1. 2:00 pmExhibition opens; the young artists stand with their work
  2. 2:30 pmWorkshops begin — they teach you how they paint
  3. 7:00 pmClose, Saturday and Sunday only

Food

Snacks, not a meal.

Before you go

Getting there
Hauz Khas (Yellow/Magenta) or Green Park.
How long
1–2 hours, or three with a workshop.
Worth knowing
2–7 pm only, Saturday and Sunday.
