Sunday Book Market

Dates1 Nov 2026
EntryFree
Time9:00 am – 11:00 am

Entirely self-guided — you squat and dig through books laid on tarpaulin. No programme, no seating.

Second-hand books of every kind, from pulp paperbacks to odd academic remainders.

When
Sun 1 Nov 2026 · 9:00 am – 11:00 am · time approximate
Where
Mahila Haat, Asaf Ali Road, New Delhi 110002
Metro
Delhi Gate · Violet · ~6 min walk
Entry
Free, no obligation to buy.
Neighbourhood
Daryaganj

What happens

Tarpaulin sheets on the ground with books stacked in rows, sellers squatting behind them. You dig, ask what a pile costs, and haggle.

Run of show

  1. 7:00 amSellers lay out tarpaulin sheets and stacks
  2. before noonThe good digging — best stock, fewest people
  3. ~2:00 pmSellers start packing up

Food

No food at the market — but Old Delhi is next door, so this is the one to pair with a proper meal.

Before you go

Getting there
Delhi Gate (Violet), about a 5-minute walk.
How long
1–2 hours if you dig properly.
Worth knowing
Before noon for the best stock. Carry cash — most sellers don't take UPI.
